# Azure Identity Management: No PIM without WIM and TIM

**Speaker(s):** Tim Hermie, Wim Matthyssen — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

In today's hybrid cloud landscape, identity has become the new perimeter. Most companies will depend on identity as their key tool to address all their challenges within their evolving cloud ecosystem, leveraging it as the catalyst of their digital transformation.

In this session, Wim and Tim will discuss a series of best practices for Azure identity management and access control security that you can use to build a secure identity perimeter. You will not only learn about PIM and emergency accounts but also how JIT and RBAC can fortify your environment's defenses.
