# MCTs make great MVPs (if we do say so ourselves)

**Speaker(s):** Jonah Andersson, Sara Fennah, Joe Griffin, Sasha Kranjac, Julian Sharp — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

It is an unfortunate truth that MCTs do not always get the recognition within Microsoft that we deserve and certainly not the same as the MVP program.  So why not aspire to be both?  As MCTs we are uniquely placed to share our expertise and become excellent MVPs.  After all, sharing our deep knowledge of the Microsoft products utilising our strong presenting and information dissemination skills is exactly what trainers do!  

In this session you hear from MCTs who are also MVPs and we'll share:
	• What the MVP program is and what it is not
	• Why become an MVP incl benefits
	• How to become an MVP

This session is an interactive session, so bring your questions too.

This is a non-technical, product agnostic session suitable for all
