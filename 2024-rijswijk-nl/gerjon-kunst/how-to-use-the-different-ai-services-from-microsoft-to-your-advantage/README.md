# How to use the different AI services from Microsoft to your advantage!

**Speaker(s):** Gerjon Kunst — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

"Is AI scary"? AI is ever present in our day-to-day work. Microsoft have a lot of AI components available. But what is the difference between co-pilot, custom copilot and Azure AI Services and how do these different services work? In this session, we'll take you on a journey to get to know them all and see usecases for the different AI services.
