# Gary Grudzinskas

<img src="https://cdn.sessionize.com/image/d438-400o400o1-9Udu2s4xSJouJNQsh72TCn.jpg" alt="Gary Grudzinskas" style="max-width:300px; max-height:300px; width:auto; height:auto;" />

Gary Grudzinskas has been an MCT for 25 years. He has trained learners in multiple mediums all over the world. Currently, Gary authors online courseware for various publishers. Gary’s field of expertise is converging technology. He has served on the Author Advisory Board at Pluralsight and organized the MCT Summit in NOLA in 2013.
