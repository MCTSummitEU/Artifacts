# Using Artificial Intelligence to Create and Deliver Courses

**Speaker(s):** Gary Grudzinskas — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence has the potential to disrupt our entire industry. This session will introduce you to AI apps that can create a lesson and deliver it with a rendered trainer. You will see a lesson completed and delivered in real time so you can see what the current technology can do and gauge how you can prepare for AI’s arrival in the training industry. All levels of experience with AI welcome.
