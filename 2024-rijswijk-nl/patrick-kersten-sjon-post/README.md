# Patrick Kersten

<img src="https://cdn.sessionize.com/image/2b32-400o400o1-p14vCSKXTuRBpUrCSCgbBh.jpg" alt="Patrick Kersten" style="max-width:300px; max-height:300px; width:auto; height:auto;" />

I am a Learning and Development professional with more than 20 years of experience. In my role as Program Director at Computrain, I am responsible for the Microsoft practice and managing the alliances with Microsoft, both locally and internationally, building powerful skills solutions together for our mutual customers and the partner ecosystem. The past years, with our (Virtual)-Instructor-Led programs and our online offerings, Computrain has become the main Cloud Skills provider in the Netherlands.

Because skilling has become more and more international I co-founded the Leading Learning Partners Association (LLPA) in 2013. At the moment, the LLPA is the biggest global IT skilling organization worldwide, with 32 members covering more than 55 countries.

# Sjon Post
