# 10 tips for more engagement!

**Speaker(s):** Gerjon Kunst, Madelon Nonkes — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

10 tips for more engagement! For everyone who stands in front of a group of people, with the goal that your story resonates with others; that they learn something, take action and become enthusiastic about it. During this session, you will receive 10 tips to ensure that your participants are engaged and stay engaged!
