# What we have learned about ransomeware attacks the last 5 years: Protect your crown jewelery!!!

**Speaker(s):** Andy Wendel — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

Over the last 5 years, we have seen a lot of ransomeware-attacks and have analyzed them. We do know the vectors, the kill chain and a lot more.
We will show, how to protect your active directory "the master way".
Active Directory can be secured till up to 97% - trust us!
We will cover how ransomeware attacks happen - LIVE!
We will talk about tier-models, ESAE ( its deprecated but still alive ), RECOBS and immutable backup.
And we will show content from our brand new book about how to highly secure ad-on-prem! :-)
