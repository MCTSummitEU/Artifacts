# Elias Markelis

<img src="https://cdn.sessionize.com/image/8fd8-400o400o1-77JS2thwXrQhH6Ks1g8ss1.JPG" alt="Elias Markelis" style="max-width:300px; max-height:300px; width:auto; height:auto;" />

After 2000 he became familiar with .NET, and became acquainted with the big gods (aka trainers) and decided to become one, desiring to try and help others become small gods in their own domains. With more than 25 years’ experience and in the past as development manager for software houses, he now acts as a freelancer trainer delivering SharePoint, SQL Server and development classes around the globe. He is also a Collaboration Architect @ Wizsp team. He has been part of various projects with a variety of technologies in the development stack of .NET, Mobility, Office 365 and SharePoint. As a technology enthusiast and SharePoint expert he delivers sessions about them at various local events as well as abroad.
