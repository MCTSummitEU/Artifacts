# Teach you students PowerShell in an hour

**Speaker(s):** Elias Markelis — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

So, you are getting to the point of the lesson where you find out that your students are either afraid of or they do not know how to use PowerShell. Now, either they are going to copy the scripts, or you can teach them. In this session we will go through a model lesson on how we can explain the basics and also some of the more advanced techniques for newcomers. What is a variable, what is the operators, what is a pipeline, how to create their own commands. Oh, and how to utilize this knowledge everywhere. As a take-away, you can take the slide deck andcr the examples, enrich them or use them as you see fit to create your own version.
