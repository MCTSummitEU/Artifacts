# Mac Management for Enterprises

**Speaker(s):** Somesh Pathak — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

macOS is the operating system that powers every MacBook. It lets you do things you can’t do with other computers. 

Although the majority of laptops/desktops we manage daily are PCs, there will still be a few Macs in your inventory that you want to manage. With Microsoft Intune, you can easily streamline device management for your macOS devices.

With this session, lets have a deep dive to explore all the possibilities of enrolling personal and company-owned MacBooks with industry-standard policies & restrictions.
