# Kenneth van Surksum

<img src="https://cdn.sessionize.com/image/c3c9-400o400o1-Bt7qFsnbJ6RRsD9kCWfjJ1.jpg" alt="Kenneth van Surksum" style="max-width:300px; max-height:300px; width:auto; height:auto;" />

Kenneth van Surksum works as a modern workplace consultant and Microsoft MVP at Itgration and is specialized in building modern workplace solutions on top of Microsoft 365. Kenneth also works with public and private cloud solutions based on Microsoft Azure and System Center. Kenneth is co-founder and board member of the Workplace Ninja User Group Netherlands and organizes (virtual) community meetings on a regular basis.

Kenneth regularly shares his knowledge by blogging at https://www.vansurksum.com and by speaking on national and international events.
