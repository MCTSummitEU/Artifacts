# From MCT to Successful YouTuber - Take your Audience to the Next Level

**Speaker(s):** Andy Malone — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

Life is all about learning, and during the dark days of Covid, Andy realised that there were folks out there that didn't have the opportunity to attend fancy schools or expensive corporate classes. So that's why Andy Malone, an MCT of 27 years created his own YouTube channel. A place where anyone can come to learn. A repository of his knowledge so that others can benefit from his experience. With a library of hundreds of videos and a huge number of subscribers from over 70 countries. You might wonder what his secret is. 
In this frank and open session, Andy will reveal his secrets on not just how you can make money on YouTube, but also grow an audience and take your MCT skills to the next level.
