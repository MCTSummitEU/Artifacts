# Beyond MOC: the future of ILT

**Speaker(s):** Julian Sharp — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

Historically MCTs deliver MOC courses for learning partners.
MOC no longer exists, we have Instructor enabled Learn content.
Many of us have seen a decrease in demand for "classic" ILT.
So, what can MCTs do.
First, I will look at creating your own courses and explain how I now generate the majority of my income from my own custom courses.
Secondly, Microsoft and their vendors use MCTs to help define exams, create questions, and write courseware. I will explain how you can earn additional income by participating such training related activities.: I will explain how each of these works, the effort required, and how you can get involved.
Questions are welcome and be prepared to challenge the speaker and add your own thoughts on the future of ILT.
