# Tim Hermie

<img src="https://cdn.sessionize.com/image/a669-400o400o1-Qk4tzmsSLFxqfpt3DvrZPe.JPG" alt="Tim Hermie" style="max-width:300px; max-height:300px; width:auto; height:auto;" />

Tim is a Technical Specialist Data Security at Microsoft BeLux. His main focus at Microsoft is showing the added value that the Microsoft Purview stack can bring to their customers concerning Risk, Compliance and Privacy. With a strong background in Security and Endpoint management over the past 10 years, Tim is able to provide a broad overview of the whole Enterprise Mobility + Security stack while showcasing how it is configured as well as showcasing how it will work for the end-users. 

Next to that Tim is a passionate Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) since 2019 and was recognized by Microsoft from 2020 till 2022 as a Microsoft MVP in the Enterprise Mobility category. Founding Board Member of the Microsoft Cloud and Client Management Community in Belgium and a frequent blogger at cloud-boy.com (old site cloud-boy.be) 
 
Tim is also recognized by Microsoft in March 2020 by receiving two digital badgers from their Azure Heroes program: Community Hero and Mentor. And in June 2020 by receiving the Most Valuable Mentor award from Microsoft.

To conclude, Tim is a techie, passionate about connecting with people and creating community in the core. If you have a question for Tim he is willing to respond to each request.
