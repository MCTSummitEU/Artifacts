# Jeroen Burgerhout

Hello, I'm Jeroen Burgerhout, a 44-year-old Microsoft enthusiast with a deep-seated passion for technology. In my professional life, I serve as a Microsoft 365 Consultant and Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT), based in the beautiful town of Nieuwkoop, Netherlands. I have over 20 years of experience primarily with Microsoft products, which allows me to bring a wealth of knowledge to my roles.

I specialize in Azure AD, Microsoft Intune, Microsoft365, Windows client, MDM, and MAM. My career has led me to work on Modern Workplace projects across a multitude of different industries. I consider myself fortunate to have the opportunity to help businesses leverage the power of Microsoft's suite of products to improve their operations.

Beyond my regular workdays, I take pride in giving back to the tech community. I host a bi-weekly show, WorkplaceDudes, where I share insights and trends related to the modern workplace. I also co-host the Microsoft 365 Modern Management Meetup, a platform that brings together like-minded professionals to discuss the latest in Microsoft 365 management. I'm an active participant in Experts Live Netherlands, where I sit on the Program Panel and serve as the Speaker Manager.

In 2020, I embarked on a new journey as an author. My first book, Microsoft Exam MD-100 Windows 10 Certification Guide, is now available. Writing this guide was a fulfilling experience as it allowed me to share my knowledge on a larger scale.

When I'm not immersed in the world of Microsoft, I enjoy exploring the Dutch landscapes (and sometimes beyond!) through geocaching. It's an activity I love to share with my girlfriend, daughter, and fellow geocaching friends.

Aside from my love for Microsoft technologies, I have a keen interest in photography. There's something uniquely satisfying about capturing the world around us through a lens. I'm also a big fan of holidays, whether it's basking in the summer sun or engaging in winter sports. And let's not forget social media – it's a fantastic way to connect with the broader community and keep up with the latest trends.

If you'd like to connect, don't hesitate to reach out. I'm always open to new opportunities and collaborations.
