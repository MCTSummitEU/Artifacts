# The Science of Communication: How to change Blah-Blahs into meaningful words!

**Speaker(s):** Sasha Kranjac — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

Without communication there is no production nor the creation and maintenance of human relations, especially those in democratic life conditions. It will not be surprising, therefore, that communication and its results draw increased attention in the everyday life practice, regardless of whether it is public or private, economic, political, or other.
Particularly, certain occupations, such as ours – Trainers’ occupation - rely on positive and successful communication process results.
Join me in this energetic, scientific, and dynamic session to understand and learn about communication process, how it works and how to use communication to improve not only as a trainer but to be better interlocutor and communicator as well!
