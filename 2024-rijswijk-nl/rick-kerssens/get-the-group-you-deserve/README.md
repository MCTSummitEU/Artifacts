# Get the group you deserve

**Speaker(s):** Rick Kerssens — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

Do you ever have a group that falls silent during interaction or a group that continues to talk while you want to finish? Do you ever have students who have trouble receiving feedback or students who don't follow your assignments?

Is this your fault or your students?

In this presentation, we're going to look at transactional analysis. We will discuss which roles you can choose as a trainer. What effect does this have on your students and how can you influence a group by varying this different roles.
