# Introduction to Teams Immersive Spaces. What MCTs need to know.

**Speaker(s):** CA Callahan — session on the [European MCT Summit 2024, Rijswijk](https://european-mct-summit-2024.sessionize.com/) agenda.

## Abstract

A brisk walk-through of what Teams Mesh Immersive Spaces are, the technical ins and outs, and a quick look at the details we might need to know in case we are called upon to use it. 

Mesh Immersive Spaces might still be in public preview and not finished baking. Details might change and improve after the session- but better to be aware of what it is like now, so you are ahead of the game when a client expects you to use it
