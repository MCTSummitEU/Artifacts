# Daniel Kordes

<img src="https://cdn.sessionize.com/image/90e5-200o200o2-1NDZtfg9czb3sm1bUFFAP.jpg" alt="Daniel Kordes" style="max-width:300px; max-height:300px; width:auto; height:auto;" />

Daniel has been working in the Microsoft Cloud as a consultant and administrator for almost 10 years. He currently works at Cyberfy Consulting AG as a Senior Expert for Microsoft 365. Daniel focuses on the Microsoft 365 infrastructure and advises companies on their path to the Microsoft Cloud and on projects relating to hybrid, Endpoint security and Intune. His passion is the community. He is a MVP for M365, speaks at conferences and has had a YouTube channel "Daniel & René M365 im Alltag" since 2022.
