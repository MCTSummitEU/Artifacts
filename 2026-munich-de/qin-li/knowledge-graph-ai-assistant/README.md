# Building a Knowledge Graph-Powered AI Assistant for Technical Training

**Speaker(s):** Qin Li — session on the [MCT DACH Community Meetup](https://mctgermany.online/mct-community-meetup/) agenda.

## Abstract

(Note: This session has also been submitted in German. I have no preferred presentation language and can present the session in either German or English.)

AI assistants are becoming increasingly capable of supporting everyday tasks, organizing information, and interacting with large amounts of knowledge and documentation. At the same time, knowledge graphs are opening new possibilities for connecting concepts, services, learning paths, and relationships across technical content.

In this session, I’ll share my experiences from building a knowledge graph-powered AI assistant for technical training using Microsoft Foundry and Microsoft Fabric.

The session focuses on the ideas, technologies, and architecture behind such a system. I will also discuss challenges encountered while experimenting with knowledge graphs and agentic AI systems for technical content, learning resources, and trainer workflows.

This session is intended for trainers, architects, AI engineers, and developers interested in practical AI assistants, knowledge graphs, and agentic systems.
